Smart Fridge AI

A camera-based food inventory system that uses YOLOv8 and OpenCV, with detection results connected to a database and web dashboard for real-time monitoring.

Computer vision

Backend

Database
Supabase (PostgreSQL) for inventory records and detection logs.
Dashboard
React and Tailwind CSS for web-based inventory monitoring.

Problem & Approach

Keeping track of refrigerator contents manually can be inconvenient. The team's prototype explores camera-based detection as a way to maintain a visible inventory of food items.

Camera frames are processed by the detection system, and item counts are passed through the backend to the database. The web dashboard presents the inventory and detection results for monitoring.

Challenges & Limitations

Lighting, camera placement, and partially hidden items can affect detection. Recognition is also limited by the food categories represented in the training data. The prototype monitors visible inventory; it does not determine food freshness or expiry.
